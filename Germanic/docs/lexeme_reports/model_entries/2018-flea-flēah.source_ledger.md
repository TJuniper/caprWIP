## Stem-class dispute note

The Orel-versus-Kroonen-versus-Kluge/Seebold disagreement here is not the
three-way notation difference seen at 'book' and 'goose': the three
dictionaries assign the word to different inflectional classes (root noun,
feminine ō-stem, masculine a-stem). The selected input `*fláuxz` follows
Orel's root-noun analysis; the entry's prose records the ō-stem and a-stem
alternatives rather than normalizing them away.
