Orel's pp.171–172, Kroonen's p.224, Ringe–Taylor's p.250, Ringe's p.152
and Fulk's pp.59,71–72 were checked against the held page images.
Ringe–Taylor's superscript j marks palatalization of w: it is not a
second independently demonstrated phoneme. The adopted encoding carries
this conditioning with ww+j / w+j; that is a disclosed computational
serialization, not a claimed resolution of the authors' phonemic question.

The adopted preference is a source-backed realized-PGmc i account, not
author counting or selection by output score. Orel and Kroonen remain
genuine published e alternatives; their different derivational explanations
are not silently harmonized. Both current fields are *xíwją; the e forms
remain actual published alternatives, not automatically earlier stages.
