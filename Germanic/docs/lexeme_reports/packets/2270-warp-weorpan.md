- Nearby heading: #### §17.21.10.2  Does breaking apply across /st/ + r?

```text
33897: The crucial distinction is **syllable structure**. Breaking applies when the vowel is in a separate syllable from the /r/: swe.os.tor (breaking) vs. stræt (no breaking, because /str/ is tautosyllabic).
33898: 
33899: **Campbell §128-141 (breaking sections)**: Campbell's treatment confirms this. Breaking applies before /r/ + C when the /r/ is **heterosyllabic** (in a different syllable from the preceding vowel). Examples: *weorčan 'work', *weorpan 'throw', *earm 'arm', *sweostor 'sister'.
33900: 
33901: **VERDICT**: Breaking DOES apply in *swéstēr → sweostor (WS), because the syllable structure is swe.os.tor, with /e/ in syllable 1 and /st/ + /r/ spanning syllables 2-3. The /r/ is heterosyllabic, so breaking applies.
```
