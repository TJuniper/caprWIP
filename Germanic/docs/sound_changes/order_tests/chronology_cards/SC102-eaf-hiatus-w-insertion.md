## Chronology statement
SC102 is the pre-OE/Anglo-Frisian hiatus-filling *w of the verba pura: `∅ -> {*w} / {*ā} _ V` at order 26, after SC028 preconsonantal *x-loss and before SC025/SC101. Per Þórhallsdóttir (1993: 114–37, apud R/T p. 151) the insertion was regular first before the *u-initial endings (pres. ind. 1sg *-u, past ind. pl. *-un) and generalized analogically through the paradigm; the executable rule models the paradigm-generalized change on citation forms. Scope is Anglo-Frisian: Old Frisian did the same (grōwinge), but OS and OHG repaired the hiatus with *j (sāian, sā(j)en), so this is not common West Germanic.

## Caveats

The regular-vs-analogical layering means the citation-form rule is a modelling generalization, documented in the FST header.
