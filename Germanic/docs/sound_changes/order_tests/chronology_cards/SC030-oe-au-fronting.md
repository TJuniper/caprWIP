## Chronology statement
Current first-break evidence places SC030 after `SC029` OE Awj Glide Formation and before `SC032` OE Diphthong Leveling. If OE Au Fronting is moved before `SC029`, PGmc `*xáwwją` yields `hauġ` rather than expected OE `hīeġ`, and PGmc `*stráwjaną` yields `strauian` rather than `strīeġan`. The later side therefore reciprocates `SC032` earlier.
