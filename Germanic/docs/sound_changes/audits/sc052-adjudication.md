## Literature

Existing dossiers: 052 hinge, 042–048 region, Anglo-Frisian synthesis and
palatalization research note.

- Fulk 2018 pp.130–132 distinguishes singleton fricatives (including initial
  g), gg/ng stops, palatal articulation, subsequent affrication and reversion.
- Ringe–Taylor 2014 pp.203–214, especially p.204, explicitly proposes merger
  of the palatal fricative with inherited j after mutation.
- Hogg 1979 pp.102–111, especially p.105, supplies key/day and objects to the
  delayed-merger solution without resolving the contradiction.
- Laker 2007 pp.166–168,175–184 separates classes and chronological layers.

CAPR chooses Ringe–Taylor's regular delayed-merger working account, not a
unanimous consensus or an account Hogg endorsed. The real fricative ʝ is a
phonetic value, never an origin tag. Stops retain disclosed eventual-reflex
proxies ʧ/ʤ; these do not assert early affrication. Broad incumbent eligibility
is retained under Fulk's broader fricative account, not silently narrowed to
Ringe–Taylor's different table.
