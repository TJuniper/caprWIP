## Regional chronology summary

The chronology cards divide this region into three different kinds of material.

1. **SC042** is narrow and witness-limited. It has a real two-sided relation, but both edges are carried by the single `rest` derivation: `SC020 < SC042 < SC043`.
2. **SC044-SC045** is the clearest adjacent positive pair. SC044 must follow SC043 and precede SC045; SC045 must follow SC044 and precede SC060. The reciprocal local core is carried by `fee`, `fight`, `flax`, `knight`, and `laugh`.
3. **SC046-SC048** is more mixed. SC046 has source-backed restoration/retraction value and real boundaries at SC043 and SC048; SC047 and SC048 form a much broader reciprocal `-en` failure core, while SC048 also keeps a narrower later link to SC059.

That means the old scaffold grouping is not the same thing as a plausible final chapter shape. The dossier question is no longer whether the region matters, but how to convert it into adjacent production units without skipping over SC043.

## Source-backed profile of the region

### SC042 as a narrow brightening-context note

SC042 `PWGmcSurvivingBimoricOUnrounding` is the most model-shaped member of the region. In CAPR it helps produce the long-vowel pathway behind `rest`, and the SC043 report already notes that its long-final brightening clause is best read as a model-specific extension of that SC042-to-SC043 path rather than as a standalone handbook headline. The handbook tradition supports the surrounding fronting/restoration corridor more readily than it isolates SC042 as an independent Old English chapter [@Campbell1959, §§131, 157--158; @HoggPhonology1992, pp. 102, 105; @RingeTaylor2014, pp. 157--158, 189--190].

That still leaves SC042 book-relevant. The chronology card gives it a genuine narrow local window, `SC020 < SC042 < SC043`, with `rest` on both sides. But the evidence is too thin and too bound to the brightening/restoration context to justify bundling it non-contiguously with SC044-SC045. The best current reading is that SC042 should eventually become either a singleton context note or a very short prefatory section immediately pointing forward to the promoted SC043 report.

### SC044-SC045 as the strongest adjacent pair

SC044 `OEBreaking` is straightforward handbook material. Campbell, Ringe and Taylor, and Fulk all treat breaking as a front-vowel development before `h`, `rC`, and `lC`, and they all place it downstream of the fronted stage created by brightening [@Campbell1959, §139; @RingeTaylor2014, §§6.2.1--6.2.3; @Fulk2018, §4.13]. The chronology card matches that source picture closely: SC044 must follow SC043 and must precede SC045.

SC045 `OEVelarFricativePalatalization` is somewhat less often isolated under exactly that CAPR label, but the underlying historical material is still standard enough. Ringe and Taylor treat palatalization of velars as a distinct OE step and then place loss of `*w` after non-initial velars after that phonologized palatalization stage [@RingeTaylor2014, §§6.4.1--6.4.2]. Campbell likewise treats the fronting of velars as later than retraction/restoration, which helps keep the local chronology legible [@Campbell1959, §§177, 405--406]. The chronology card gives the strongest practical reason to pair it with SC044: `SC044 < SC045` is directly reciprocal through the `fee`, `fight`, `flax`, `knight`, and `laugh` witness set, while the right side points much later to SC060 only by a single `six` witness.

Taken together, SC044-SC045 is the cleanest near-term adjacent production candidate in the region. It has real handbook backing, a tight reciprocal local card pair, and a natural book role immediately to the right of the already promoted SC043 report.

### SC046 as a real restoration hinge

SC046 `OEARestoration` is not as narrow as SC042 and not as locally reciprocal as SC044-SC045, but it is solidly source-backed. Campbell's restoration of `a` before following back vowels, and Ringe and Taylor's general retraction of fronted low vowels, describe the same historical corridor that CAPR isolates here [@Campbell1959, §§157--159; @RingeTaylor2014, §6.3.1; @Fulk2018, §4.13]. The card evidence is also intelligible: SC046 must follow SC043 and must precede SC048, with `bake`, `fare`, `grave`, `lade`, and `wade` giving the core failures.

That makes SC046 a legitimate chapter member. The open question is structural rather than evidential: does it belong in one cautious adjacent `SC046-SC048` corridor, or should it eventually stand apart from the tighter SC047-SC048 nasal-tail core?

### SC047-SC048 as a broad nasal-tail core

SC047 `OEHeavySyllableNasalApocope` and SC048 `OESecondaryNasalization` are the least textbook-like part of the region, but the cards show that they interact strongly inside CAPR. SC047 has only a thin earlier anchor at SC034, yet its later boundary across SC048 produces an 87-row reciprocal `-en` failure set. SC048 mirrors that same earlier boundary and then keeps a narrower later relation to SC059 through `steal` and `weave`.

The source sweep found less clean standalone handbook chapter support here than it did for SC044-SC045 or SC046. Campbell's sections on loss of nasals and back mutation are relevant context, but they do not map one-to-one onto CAPR's pair [@Campbell1959, §403, §§205--206]. Ringe and Taylor are helpful mainly on the later back-mutation side: they explicitly distinguish back umlaut from earlier fronting and i-umlaut strata, which makes SC048's rightward relation to SC059 historically plausible even if SC047-SC048 is still more model-shaped than source-headline material [@RingeTaylor2014, §6.9.4].

The practical result is that SC047-SC048 looks like a real computational corridor, but one that still needs cautious narration. If it is promoted, the prose will have to say plainly that the reciprocal `-en` failure core is broad and important inside the model even though the handbook tradition does not usually package these two rules as a classic named pair.

## Chapter-shape options

### Option 1: SC042 singleton or short context note

This is now the best reading of SC042.

- **Why it works:** it preserves the real `rest`-based relation `SC020 < SC042 < SC043` without pretending SC042 is a major chapter.
- **Why it should not stay in `042-044-045` as a future full unit:** any unchanged full promotion would skip over already promoted SC043, violating the adjacent-only assembly rule.
- **Recommended framing:** a brief context note or singleton that points directly to SC043 as the real brightening pivot.

### Option 2: SC044-SC045 adjacent pair

This is the strongest near-term promotion target in the region.

- **Why it works:** it is strictly adjacent, source-backed, and card-supported in both directions.
- **Core chronology:** `SC043 < SC044 < SC045 < SC060`, with the strong local reciprocal evidence concentrated in `SC044 <-> SC045`.
- **Recommended framing:** an adjacent breaking and velar-fricative palatalization report that cross-references SC043 on the left and SC060 on the right without turning either cross-reference into part of the unit.

### Option 3: SC046-SC048 cautious corridor

This remains viable.

- **Why it works:** it preserves adjacency and keeps three ordinary FST changes visible in order.
- **Why it needs caution:** the source tradition is uneven across the three members. SC046 is historically easy to narrate; SC047-SC048 is more computationally than canonically paired.
- **Recommended framing if chosen:** a cautious corridor report centered on restoration feeding into a broad nasal-tail cleanup zone, with explicit notice that SC047-SC048 is the tighter internal core.

### Option 4: SC046 singleton plus SC047-SC048 pair

This is the main alternative still left open.

- **Why it works:** it matches the evidence profile well. SC046 has its own restoration/retraction identity, while SC047-SC048 has the strongest internal reciprocal card relation in the corridor.
- **Why it is not recommended yet:** the present task is review and dossier preparation only, not row-splitting. More importantly, the source work does not yet prove that SC047-SC048 alone would read better as prose than a cautious SC046-SC048 corridor.
- **Current judgment:** keep this as a live human-review option rather than a settled recommendation.

## Working recommendation

The regional review now supports the following priority order.

1. **Promote SC044-SC045 first** if the next substantive step is a production report. This is the strongest adjacent and source-backed pair in the post-brightening region.
2. **Then decide the SC046-SC048 shape**: either keep the cautious corridor or split later into SC046 plus an SC047-SC048 nasal-tail pair.
