## Historical problem

`SC079-SC087` is the terminal visibility scaffold at the end of the current
sound-change half. It clearly does **not** behave like one eight-change future
production chapter. Instead, the cards and the source tradition point toward
four different kinds of closing material:

1. a final-`j` / final-geminate opening bridge (`SC079-SC080`);
2. a `j`-strengthening / vocalization / unstressed-`ei` chain (`SC081-SC083`);
3. a more standard `h`-loss / contraction core (`SC085-SC086`);
4. an independent terminal `r`-metathesis note (`SC087`).

The closing dossier question is therefore not whether the region matters, but
how it should later be split into smaller scaffold rows before any promotion.

- short quotation:

  > "In early OE a marked tendency is to be observed for g to be vocalized ..."

  > "Most cases are due to the loss of h between diphthong and vowel ..."

- paraphrase: Campbell is strongest for the middle and right side of the cluster. His contraction and vocalization sections directly support the `SC081-SC083` and `SC085-SC086` material through forms such as `stréide`, `céide`, `téon`, `sléan`, `séon`, and `téo` [@Campbell1959, §§234--239, 266--274, 731]. - conditioning: vocalization and contraction arise after loss of `h` or glide material; final geminates simplify in late OE orthographic/phonological practice

- examples: `stréide`; `géide`; `téon`; `sléan`; `séon`; `foh`; final geminates
- cautions: Campbell gives weaker direct support for SC079 as a named chapter center and little reason to treat the full eight-change row as one coherent unit

## CAPR formulation versus literature

The source tradition does **not** support the current grouped row as one future
chapter. It supports at least three different closing architectures:

1. a smaller `SC079-SC080` final-`j` / degemination bridge;
2. a technically coherent `SC081-SC083` vocalization/contraction bridge with
   SC082 as its center;
3. a stronger and more book-facing `SC085-SC086` h-loss/contraction core;
4. an independent terminal `SC087` metathesis note.

That means the current scaffold is primarily a terminal visibility placeholder,
not a promotable production unit.
