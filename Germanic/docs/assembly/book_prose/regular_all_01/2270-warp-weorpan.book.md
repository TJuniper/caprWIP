Ringe and Taylor give preterite _\*warp_ [@RingeTaylor2014, 195] and infinitive _\*werpana_ [@RingeTaylor2014, 197]. The selected input here is the verbal form _\*wérpaną_. Clark Hall records _weorpan_ as the strong verb headword and separately lists _wearp_ as both noun and preterite. Breaking before _r + C_ yields _weor-_, and the infinitive develops regularly to _weorpan_ [@Campbell1959, §146; @HoggPhonology1992, §3.4.2.2]. English *warp* also points to related _wearp_ material. The selected form here is the infinitive _weorpan_.
